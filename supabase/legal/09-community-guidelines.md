# Community Guidelines

**Plan E** · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Plan E connects travellers with local hosts across Nepal. These guidelines apply to
everyone — travellers, hosts, and guides — on the platform and on the experiences
themselves.

---

## 1. The basics

**Be honest.** Real names, real details, real listings, real reviews.
**Be respectful.** To each other, to communities, to the country.
**Be safe.** Follow safety instructions. Speak up when something is wrong.
**Be reliable.** Turn up. Communicate. Do what you said you would.

## 2. For travellers

### Before

- Give accurate details for everyone in your party
- Disclose medical conditions, injuries, allergies and fitness limits relevant to the
  activity. This is safety information, not personal admin
- Read the listing properly — inclusions, requirements, what to bring, difficulty
- Ask questions before booking rather than assuming

### During

- Arrive on time at the stated meeting point
- Follow the guide's instructions, especially on safety. On a mountain the guide's
  judgment is final
- Bring the required equipment. Hosts can refuse participation without refund if you
  do not
- Respect your group — pace, decisions and shared conditions affect everyone
- Look after the environment. Carry out what you carry in. Stay on marked trails
- Respect local culture: ask before photographing people, dress appropriately at
  religious sites, remove shoes where expected, and follow the customs of the community
  hosting you

### After

- Review honestly, based on your actual experience
- Raise problems with the host first, and with us if unresolved

## 3. For hosts

### Your listings

- Describe accurately. Real photos of your actual experience, not stock imagery of
  somewhere else
- State the real difficulty. Understating it is a safety issue, not marketing
- List inclusions and exclusions clearly, and required equipment fully
- Keep availability and pricing current

### Your operation

- Hold every licence, permit and registration the activity requires under Nepali law,
  including Nepal Tourism Board registration and trekking or rafting licensing where
  applicable
- Use qualified, trained guides at the ratios the activity demands
- Maintain equipment and replace what is worn
- Carry appropriate first aid, and communication capability where you operate beyond
  network coverage
- Follow the Safety and Risk Policy in full

### Your conduct

- Respond to booking messages within **24 hours**
- Honour confirmed bookings. Repeated cancellations lead to removal
- Never solicit off-platform payment for a Plan E booking
- Treat travellers professionally regardless of nationality, gender, religion, caste,
  ethnicity, disability, sexual orientation or age
- Pay and treat your staff — guides, porters, drivers — fairly. Porter welfare standards
  including load limits, equipment and insurance are a condition of listing on Plan E

## 4. Reviews

- Review only experiences you actually took
- Describe your own experience — not rumour, not another group's
- No personal attacks, no discriminatory language, no revealing others' personal details
- No paid, incentivised or traded reviews, in either direction. Hosts must not offer
  discounts or gifts for positive reviews or ask for a review to be removed as a
  condition of resolving a complaint

We remove reviews that break these rules, and we act against accounts that manipulate
them.

## 5. Messaging

Trip chat is for coordinating the experience. No harassment, no spam, no unsolicited
commercial messages, no sharing of another person's contact details or private
information without consent.

Messages may be reviewed by moderators when reported.

## 6. Not allowed, at all

- Illegal activity of any kind, including drug use and wildlife crime
- Sexual harassment, unwanted advances, or any sexual conduct involving a minor
- Violence or threats
- Discrimination on any ground
- Fake accounts, impersonation, fraudulent bookings, payment fraud
- Circumventing Plan E to avoid fees after using the platform to find each other
- Damaging cultural, religious or natural heritage
- Endangering others through reckless conduct

## 7. Reporting

**In the app:** report on a listing, profile, message or review
**Email:** [SUPPORT EMAIL] · **Urgent:** [EMERGENCY HOTLINE]

Reports of harassment, assault or discrimination are treated urgently and
confidentially. We will not disclose your identity to the person reported without your
agreement, unless legally compelled.

## 8. What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity: a warning, content removal, listing suspension, restricted
features, or permanent removal. Serious cases — safety, harassment, fraud, anything
involving a minor — go straight to removal and, where appropriate, to Nepal Police.

You will normally be told what rule was broken and given a chance to respond, except
where doing so would put someone at risk. Decisions can be appealed through the
Grievance Policy.

## 9. These guidelines change

Nepal's tourism sector changes, and so will these. Material updates are notified in the
app.
