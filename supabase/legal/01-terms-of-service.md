## 2. What Plan E is, and what it is not

Plan E is a **booking platform**. We connect travellers with independent local hosts
who organise and deliver experiences — treks, tours, workshops, homestays, wellness
sessions, volunteering and similar activities.

**We do not operate the experiences ourselves.** When you book, you enter into a
contract for the experience with the host, not with Plan E. Plan E's role is to:

- list experiences and their details as supplied by hosts
- take your booking and collect payment on the host's behalf
- pass your booking to the host and provide the tools to manage it
- provide customer support and a grievance process

We do our best to check who our hosts are and to require appropriate licences,
but we do not supervise experiences as they happen. What this means for
responsibility is set out in section 12 and in the Safety and Risk Policy.
