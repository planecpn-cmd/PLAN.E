# Refund Policy

**Plan E** · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This explains how refunds are calculated, paid and timed. What you are **entitled** to
is set by the Cancellation Policy.

---

## 1. What can be refunded

| Component | Refundable? |
|---|---|
| Host experience price | Per the Cancellation Policy tiers |
| Plan E service fee (5%) | Not refundable if you cancel by choice. Fully refundable if the host or Plan E cancels, or on force majeure |
| Payment gateway charges | Refunded where the gateway returns them; otherwise deducted and disclosed |
| Third-party costs already paid (permits, park fees, non-refundable flights) | Only if genuinely recoverable. Non-recoverable amounts must be itemised and evidenced by the host |

## 2. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back **to the original payment method** — the same Khalti or eSewa account
used for the booking. We cannot redirect a refund to a different account, person or
method. This is a fraud-prevention requirement, not a preference.

If the original method is closed or unreachable, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will
arrange an alternative after identity verification, which takes longer.

## 3. Timing

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| We approve or query your request | Within **3 working days** |
| We initiate the refund with the gateway | Within **7 working days** of approval |
| Money reaches your account | Typically **5–10 working days** after initiation, depending on the gateway and your bank |

Total: usually within **15 working days**, and in all cases within **30 days**, as
required by consumer protection rules.

We will tell you the date we initiated the refund and the reference. After that the
timing is with your payment provider and bank.

## 4. Partial refunds

Where only part is refundable, we send you a written breakdown before processing:
amount paid, refundable portion, each deduction with its reason, and the final amount.
Deductions we cannot explain, we do not make.

## 5. Credits instead of cash

Where offered, a Plan E credit:

- is valid **12 months** from issue
- is usable on any experience on the platform
- is often **higher in value** than the cash refund would be, particularly for force
  majeure cancellations
- is non-transferable and cannot later be converted to cash

Accepting a credit is your choice. We will not present a credit as the only option where
a cash refund is due.

## 6. Failed and duplicate payments

**Money left your account but no booking reference appeared.** The transaction likely
failed after debit. Contact us with the date, amount and gateway reference. Most
auto-reverse within 3–5 working days. If not, we escalate to the gateway and keep you
updated until resolved. You are not required to chase the gateway yourself.

**Charged twice for one booking.** Report it and we refund the duplicate in full,
including the service fee, within 7 working days. No cancellation tier applies — you
were never entitled to be charged twice.

## 7. Refunds after a complaint

Where an experience was materially not as described, or was not delivered, and this is
upheld under the Grievance Policy, a refund may be granted outside the standard tiers —
partial or full depending on what was actually delivered.

We assess: what the listing promised, what happened, whether you raised it at the time,
and evidence from both sides. Findings are given in writing with reasons.

## 8. Chargebacks

Please come to us first. A chargeback filed without contacting us takes longer and
freezes the booking while the gateway investigates.

Where you have raised a legitimate issue and we have failed to respond within our stated
timelines, filing a chargeback is entirely your right, and we will not penalise your
account for it.

## 9. If we get it wrong

If we make an error in a refund calculation, we correct it and pay the difference — with
no time limit and no requirement that you spotted it within a certain window.

## 10. Escalation

Unhappy with a refund decision: **Grievance Policy** → written review within 7 working
days → escalation to [GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. Beyond that you may approach the Department of
Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection. Using our process does not waive that right.
