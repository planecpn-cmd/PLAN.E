## 1. Who you are contracting with

When you book, you enter into a contract **with the host** for the delivery of that
experience. Plan E facilitates the booking and collects payment as the host's limited
collection agent.

## 2. How a booking is made

1. You select an experience, a departure date, and the number of adults and children
2. You provide contact details and any safety information the host requires
3. You review the price breakdown — host price, service fee, total
4. You pay through Khalti or eSewa
5. On successful payment we issue a **booking reference** and a confirmation

**Your booking is confirmed only when you receive a booking reference.** Payment alone,
or a pending gateway screen, is not confirmation. If money leaves your account without a
reference appearing, contact us immediately — see the Payment Policy.

## 3. Price quotes

Prices at checkout are held for **15 minutes**. After that the quote expires and is
recalculated, because availability and host pricing can change.

The total includes the host's price for your party plus Plan E's service fee of **5%**.
Both are itemised before payment.

## 4. What the price includes

Only what the listing's "What's Included" section says. Unless listed, assume the price
does **not** cover: international or domestic flights, travel insurance, visa fees,
national park and conservation area entry, TIMS or trekking permits, personal equipment,
tips, or meals not specified.

The listing's "What to Bring" section is the host's requirement, not a suggestion. A
host may refuse participation if you arrive without required equipment, and no refund is
due in that case.

## 5. Availability and spots

Availability shown is the host's. Where fewer than 5 spots remain the listing says so.
Spots are allocated on payment, not on starting the booking form.

Some experiences have a **minimum group size**. If it is not met, the host may cancel —
treated as a host cancellation under the Cancellation Policy, with a full refund.

## 6. Your responsibilities

You confirm that:

- The details you gave are accurate, including the names and ages of everyone in your party
- You have disclosed medical conditions, injuries, allergies and fitness limitations
  relevant to the activity
- You meet the minimum age and any fitness or experience requirement in the listing
- You will arrive at the meeting point at the stated time
- You hold valid identification and, where required, permits and travel insurance
- You have read the Safety and Risk Policy for the activity type

**Late arrival or no-show is treated as a traveller cancellation with no refund.**

## 7. Changes by you

Requests to change a date, guest count or participant name are made through the app or
[SUPPORT EMAIL], and are subject to the host's availability and agreement. An approved
change may carry a price difference. A change request is not confirmed until you receive
confirmation of it.

If a change cannot be accommodated, the Cancellation Policy applies.

## 8. Changes by the host

Hosts may make minor changes — a shifted start time, a substituted guide, a re-routed
itinerary for safety. We tell you as soon as we know.

If the host makes a **material** change — a different date, a substantially different
itinerary, a materially different location, or a price increase — you may accept it, or
cancel for a **full refund**.

## 9. Cancellation

See the **Cancellation Policy** and **Refund Policy**.

## 10. Health, fitness and participation

Hosts may set fitness, age, medical or experience requirements. A host may refuse or
stop your participation where they reasonably believe continuing would be unsafe for you
or the group.

If refusal follows from something you failed to disclose, no refund is due. If it
follows from a requirement not stated in the listing, it is a host cancellation with a
full refund.

## 11. Multi-day and package experiences

For treks, expeditions and multi-day trips:

- The itinerary is indicative. Guides may vary it for weather, trail conditions, altitude
  or group welfare, and their judgment is final on safety
- Accommodation is as described, and teahouse standards vary
- Permit and park fees are your responsibility unless listed as included
- Travel insurance covering **helicopter evacuation** is strongly recommended for any
  trek above 3,000 m, and is mandatory where the listing says so

## 12. Complaints

Raise problems with the host **during** the experience where possible — most are fixable
on the spot. If unresolved, use the Grievance Policy. Complaints raised more than
**30 days** after the experience are harder to investigate and may not be actionable.
