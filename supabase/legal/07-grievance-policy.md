## 2. What you can complain about

Anything: an experience that was not as described, a host's conduct, a refund decision,
a payment problem, your data, another user's behaviour, safety, or how we handled an
earlier complaint.

## 3. How to complain

**In the app:** Profile → Help & Support → Contact Us
**Email:** [GRIEVANCE EMAIL]
**Phone:** [SUPPORT PHONE]
**Post:** Grievance Officer, [REGISTERED ADDRESS]

Please include, where you have them: your booking reference, what happened and when,
what you would like as an outcome, and any photos, screenshots or messages.

A complaint without all of this is still valid — we will ask for what we need. We will
not reject a complaint for being incomplete.

## 4. Timelines

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement, with a reference number | **1 working day** |
| Substantive response | **7 working days** |
| Complex cases needing investigation | Up to **21 working days**, with progress updates every 7 |
| Escalation review decision | **7 working days** from escalation |

If we cannot meet a timeline, we tell you before it expires, with a reason and a revised
date.

## 5. How we handle it

1. **Acknowledge** — reference number and named handler
2. **Gather** — your account, the host's account, booking records, messages, payment logs
3. **Assess** — against the listing as it appeared when you booked, and against these
   policies
4. **Decide** — in writing, with reasons, stating what we found and what we will do
5. **Act** — refund, credit, host warning or removal, policy change, apology

We decide on the evidence. We do not automatically side with the host, and we do not
automatically side with the traveller.

## 6. Escalation

Unhappy with the outcome, reply within **30 days** asking for escalation. A senior
person **not involved in the original decision** reviews it and responds within 7
working days. That decision is our final position.

## 7. Outside Plan E

Using our process is not a precondition and does not waive any right. You may at any
time approach:

- **Department of Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection**, Government of Nepal
- **Nepal Tourism Board** — for licensed tourism operator conduct
- **Nepal Rastra Bank** or your gateway — for payment disputes
- **National Information Commission** — for privacy and data matters
- **Nepal Police** — for any criminal matter
- The **courts of Kathmandu**

## 8. Urgent and safety matters

Anything involving **immediate danger, an ongoing incident, harassment, or a threat to
safety** does not wait for these timelines. Call [EMERGENCY HOTLINE] or use the in-app
emergency contact. See the Emergency Policy.

Reports of sexual harassment, assault, discrimination or exploitation are treated as
urgent, handled confidentially by a senior member of staff, and may result in immediate
suspension of the account complained about while we investigate.

## 9. Complaints about a traveller

Hosts may complain about travellers through the same process, with the same timelines
and the same standard of evidence.

## 10. Records and transparency

Complaints are logged and retained for **3 years**. You may request a copy of your own
complaint file at any time.

We review complaint patterns quarterly and use them to change hosts, listings and
policies. Repeated upheld complaints against a host lead to removal.

## 11. Bad faith

We may decline to process complaints that are abusive, repetitive after a final
decision, or knowingly false. We will say so in writing with reasons, and you keep every
external right in section 7.
