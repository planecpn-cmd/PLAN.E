## What you are acknowledging

By confirming this booking, I acknowledge that:

### 1. The activity carries inherent risk

Adventure and outdoor activities in Nepal involve risks that cannot be eliminated even
when everyone acts correctly. Depending on the activity these include:

- Falls, slips, and injury on uneven, steep, icy or exposed terrain
- **Altitude sickness**, including its severe forms affecting the lungs or brain, which
  can be fatal and can affect anyone regardless of fitness or prior experience
- Avalanche, rockfall, landslide, and crevasse hazard
- Extreme cold, heat, sun exposure, dehydration, hypothermia and frostbite
- Drowning, capsize and impact on water-based activities
- Equipment failure despite maintenance
- Road accidents, including on mountain roads
- Animal encounters, insect-borne illness and waterborne illness
- Sudden and severe weather change
- **Delayed rescue.** In remote areas, help may be hours or days away. Helicopter
  evacuation depends on weather, daylight and availability, and may be impossible when
  most needed
- Limited medical facilities outside major towns
- Serious injury, permanent disability, or death

### 2. I have assessed myself honestly

- I have read the listing, the stated difficulty, the itinerary and the requirements
- I believe I am physically and mentally capable of this activity
- I have disclosed all relevant medical conditions, injuries, allergies, medications and
  limitations
- Where relevant, I have consulted a doctor
- I understand that concealing a relevant condition endangers me and my group, and may
  result in refusal of participation without refund

### 3. I will follow instructions

- The guide's decisions on safety, including turning back or changing route, are final
- I will report symptoms — especially altitude symptoms — immediately and honestly
- I will bring the required equipment
- I will not pressure guides to continue against their judgment

### 4. I understand the insurance position

- **Plan E does not provide insurance**
- Emergency evacuation, including helicopter rescue, is at my own or my insurer's cost
- I am responsible for holding adequate cover for this specific activity and altitude
- Where the listing requires insurance, I confirm I hold it

### 5. I understand who does what

- The **host** organises and delivers this experience and is responsible for its safe
  conduct
- **Plan E** operates the booking platform, verifies hosts and licences at listing, and
  is not present on the experience
- Both remain responsible for taking reasonable care in their own roles. Nothing here
  changes that

### 6. Conditions may change

The itinerary may be altered or the activity abandoned for weather, conditions, or group
welfare. This is a safety decision, and refund treatment follows the Cancellation
Policy.
