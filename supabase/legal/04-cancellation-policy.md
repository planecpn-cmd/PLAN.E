# Cancellation Policy

**Plan E** · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

How cancellation works depends on **who** cancels and **when**. This policy sets the
entitlement; the Refund Policy sets out how the money is returned.

---

## 1. Cancelling as a traveller

Cancel through **Plans → your booking → Cancel**, or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL].
The cancellation takes effect when we acknowledge it in writing, not when you decide.

### Standard tiers

Timing is measured from the experience **start time** shown on your booking.

| You cancel | You receive |
|---|---|
| More than 7 days before | **100%** of the experience price |
| 3 to 7 days before | **50%** of the experience price |
| Less than 3 days before | **No refund** |
| After it has started, or no-show | **No refund** |

The **Plan E service fee (5%) is non-refundable** where you cancel by choice, because
the booking was processed. It is fully refunded where the host or Plan E cancels, or
where a force majeure event prevents the experience.

### Where a host sets different terms

Some experiences — multi-day treks, expeditions, permit-dependent trips, small-group
departures — carry stricter host terms. Where they do, **the listing shows them on the
experience page and at checkout before you pay**, and those terms apply instead.

A host's terms can be stricter than the standard tiers but must be disclosed
pre-purchase. Terms not disclosed before payment cannot be applied to you.

## 2. Cancellation by the host

If a host cancels for any reason, you receive a **100% refund including the service
fee**. Where possible we will also offer an alternative date or a comparable experience.

Common host cancellation reasons: minimum group size not met, guide illness or injury,
equipment failure, permit refusal, or a safety decision.

Where a host cancels **within 48 hours** of the start time and the reason is within
their control, we will also cover reasonable, documented, non-recoverable costs you
incurred in reliance on the booking — such as a non-refundable domestic flight or the
first night of accommodation — up to the value of the booking. Receipts required.

## 3. Cancellation by Plan E

We may cancel a booking where we find a listing to be fraudulent or materially
misrepresented, where a host loses a required licence, or where we reasonably believe
proceeding would be unsafe or unlawful. Full refund including the service fee.

## 4. Weather, natural events and force majeure

Nepal's conditions are what they are. Flights to mountain airstrips are cancelled
routinely, trails close, and monsoon disrupts schedules.

Where an experience cannot go ahead, or must be abandoned, because of an event outside
anyone's control — extreme weather, landslide, avalanche, flood, earthquake, epidemic,
strike or bandh, civil unrest, government restriction, permit suspension, or airport or
road closure — the following applies:

**Cancelled before it starts:**
- **Full refund** of everything recoverable, including the service fee
- Genuinely non-recoverable third-party costs the host has already paid on your behalf —
  permits, non-refundable flights, advance lodge deposits — may be deducted. The host
  must **itemise and evidence** each deduction. Unevidenced deductions are not permitted
- You may instead take a **credit valid 12 months** for the full amount, with no
  deduction

**Abandoned after it starts:**
- A refund proportionate to the unused portion, less costs already incurred
- Costs of evacuation or of returning you to a safe location are covered by you or your
  insurance, unless the listing states otherwise

**We strongly recommend travel insurance covering trip cancellation, interruption and
emergency evacuation.** Plan E does not provide insurance.

## 5. Illness and personal emergencies

Cancellation for personal illness, injury, bereavement or family emergency falls under
the standard tiers in section 1 — we cannot waive the host's costs for reasons on your
side. **This is what travel insurance is for.**

We will, on request and with documentation, provide a written confirmation of your
booking and cancellation to support an insurance claim.

Where you cancel late for a documented medical reason, we will ask the host to offer a
date change or credit as a goodwill measure. Hosts are encouraged but not obliged to
agree.

## 6. Group bookings

Removing individuals from a group booking is treated as a partial cancellation of the
removed spots, under the same tiers. If removals take the group below the host's minimum
size, the host may cancel the whole departure — treated as a host cancellation for the
remaining travellers.

## 7. Drafts and unpaid bookings

A booking in draft state carries no obligation and can be deleted at any time with no
charge. Nothing is reserved until payment succeeds.

## 8. Disputes

If you believe a cancellation outcome is wrong, raise it through the **Grievance
Policy**. We review the listing terms as they appeared when you booked, the timing, and
the evidence from both sides.
