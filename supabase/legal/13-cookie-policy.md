# Cookie Policy

**Plan E website** (planE website) · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This applies to the Plan E **website only**. The mobile app does not use cookies, but
uses local storage and device identifiers for equivalent purposes — see the Privacy
Policy.

---

## 1. What cookies are

Small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let a site
remember you between pages and visits. We also use local storage and similar
technologies; "cookies" here covers all of them.

## 2. What we use, and why

### Strictly necessary — always on

Required for the site to function. These cannot be turned off, and do not require your
consent.

| Purpose | What it does | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication | Keeps you signed in | Session, or 30 days if "remember me" |
| Security | CSRF protection, abuse prevention | Session |
| Booking state | Holds your booking in progress and the 15-minute price quote | Session |
| Load balancing | Routes your request to a working server | Session |
| Consent record | Remembers your cookie choices | 12 months |

### Functional — with your consent

| Purpose | What it does | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Preferences | Language and currency selection | 12 months |
| Recently viewed | Shows experiences you looked at | 30 days |
| Approximate location | Shows nearby experiences | Session |

### Analytics — with your consent

| Purpose | What it does | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Usage analytics | Which pages are used, where people drop off | Up to 24 months |
| Performance | Load times, errors, crashes | Up to 24 months |

Analytics data is aggregated. We use it to fix and improve the site, not to build
advertising profiles.

### Advertising — we do not use them

**Plan E does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.** We do not run
retargeting pixels, and we do not share your browsing data with advertisers or data
brokers.

If this ever changes, we will ask for your consent first — it will not be switched on
quietly.

## 4. Your choices

**On the banner:** on your first visit you can accept all, reject all non-essential, or
choose by category. **Rejecting is a single click, equally prominent as accepting** —
you are not required to click through menus to decline.

**Later:** change your choices any time via "Cookie settings" in the site footer.

**In your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in browser settings. Blocking
strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in and booking from working.

Rejecting functional and analytics cookies does not restrict any feature of the site or
change what you pay.

## 5. Do Not Track

We honour browser Do Not Track signals by not loading analytics cookies when one is
present.

Material changes are announced on the site and, where required, we will re-request
