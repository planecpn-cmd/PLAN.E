# Payment Policy

**Plan E** · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

---

## 1. Currency

All prices and payments are in **Nepalese Rupees (NPR)**. Prices display in Nepali
numbering format (e.g. Rs. 14,50,000).

If you pay from a foreign card or wallet, your provider converts at their rate and may
add a foreign transaction fee. Plan E neither sets nor receives any part of that. The
amount you are charged in your own currency may differ from the NPR figure shown.

## 2. Accepted methods

- **Khalti** (default)
- **eSewa**

Both are licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank. Payment happens inside the provider's own secure
flow. Plan E never sees or stores your card number, CVV, PIN, or wallet credentials.

Cash on arrival appears in the app as a saved preference but is **not available for
online bookings** unless a listing explicitly offers it.

## 3. What you pay

| Line | Description |
|---|---|
| Experience price | Host's price × adults, plus children at the host's child rate |
| Plan E service fee | **5%** of the experience price |
| **Total** | Charged in one transaction |

Every line is itemised at checkout before you pay. There are no charges added after
payment.

## 4. The service fee

The 5% service fee covers payment processing, platform operation, customer support and
the grievance process. It is charged to the traveller, shown separately, and is
**non-refundable where you cancel by choice** — the work it pays for has been done.

It **is** refunded in full where the host or Plan E cancels, or where force majeure
prevents the experience.

## 5. Quote validity

A checkout quote is valid **15 minutes**, shown as a countdown. On expiry the quote is
recalculated against current availability and pricing. This protects both you and the
host from stale prices.

## 6. When you are charged

Once, at booking, for the full amount. Plan E does not take deposits, instalments or
recurring charges. Nothing is stored for future automatic billing.

## 7. Confirmation

A booking is confirmed when you receive a **booking reference**. If you are charged
without receiving one, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the date, amount and gateway
reference. See the Refund Policy, section 6 — we handle the gateway escalation.

## 8. Payments outside the platform

Do not pay a host directly for a booking made through Plan E, and do not accept a host's
request to move a Plan E booking off-platform.

Off-platform payments have **no booking record, no refund protection, no grievance
route and no dispute support**. We cannot help recover money paid this way. Hosts who
solicit this are removed from the platform — please report it.

This does not cover genuinely separate on-the-ground costs — tips, personal purchases,
optional extras arranged on the day. Those are between you and the host.

## 9. Taxes

Prices include applicable Nepali taxes where the host and Plan E are liable. VAT and
service tax are reflected in the total. Tax invoices are available on request at
[SUPPORT EMAIL].

Host tax obligations on their earnings are the host's own responsibility.

## 10. Host payouts

Hosts are paid to the bank account or wallet registered during verification, in NPR,
net of the platform's commission and any applicable withholding.

Payouts are released **[PAYOUT TIMING — CONFIRM: e.g. within X working days after the
experience has been delivered]**. Holding payout until after delivery protects the
traveller's refund entitlement.

Payout may be withheld pending an open grievance, a suspected fraudulent booking, or a
disputed transaction, until the matter is resolved. Hosts are told when and why.

## 11. Security

TLS in transit, encryption at rest. No card or wallet credentials stored on Plan E
systems. Transactions monitored for fraud patterns; suspicious ones are reviewed by a
person before any action.

Report a payment you do not recognise immediately to [SUPPORT EMAIL] and to your
gateway provider.

## 12. Errors

If you are charged the wrong amount through our error, we refund the difference in full,
promptly, on our own initiative once identified — you do not need to have noticed it.

escalate.
