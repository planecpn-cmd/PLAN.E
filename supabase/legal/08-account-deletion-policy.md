# Account Deletion Policy and Procedure

**Plan E** · Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

You can delete your Plan E account at any time. This explains how, what happens, and
what we must keep.

---

## 1. How to delete your account

**In the app:** Profile → Settings → Delete Account
**On the web:** Profile → Settings → Delete Account
**By email:** [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your registered address, subject "Account Deletion"

The in-app route is available without contacting support, without an explanation, and
without a retention offer blocking the path. App store policy and Nepali data protection
law both require deletion to be as easy as sign-up.

## 2. Before you can delete

You cannot delete while you have:

- an **upcoming confirmed booking** — cancel it first (Cancellation Policy applies) or
  wait until it completes
- an **open grievance or dispute** — it must conclude first
- an **unresolved payment or refund** — it must settle first
- as a host: **upcoming bookings from travellers**, or an unpaid balance

We tell you which of these applies and what to do. Once cleared, deletion proceeds.

## 3. What happens, and when

| Stage | Timing |
|---|---|
| Request received, confirmation email sent | Immediately |
| **Grace period** — you can cancel the request by logging in | **7 days** |
| Account deactivated, profile no longer visible, login disabled | Day 7 |
| Personal data erased or anonymised | **Within 30 days** of the request |
| Removal from backups | Within **90 days**, as backup cycles rotate |

## 4. What is deleted

- Name, email, phone, profile photo, bio, location
- Interests and personalisation data
- Saved experiences
- Notification preferences and device tokens
- Search and browsing history
- Health, dietary and emergency contact information
- Direct messages (subject to section 5)

## 5. What is retained, and why

Some data cannot be deleted on request because law requires us to keep it.

| Data | Retained | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Booking and payment records — reference, amount, date, tax details | **7 years** | Tax and company law |
| Records relating to a dispute, claim or investigation | Until concluded, plus limitation period | Legal claims |
| Records of a safety incident | **7 years** | Safety, insurance, legal duty |
| Anonymised reviews | Indefinitely | Legitimate interest — other travellers relied on them |
| Suppression record — a hash of your email | Indefinitely | To honour your deletion and not re-contact you |
| Messages with a host about a completed booking | 2 years | Dispute resolution |

**Retained records are locked down**: separated from your profile, restricted to staff
who need them, and not used for marketing, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

## 6. Your reviews

By default, reviews are **anonymised** rather than removed — the text stays, your name
and photo are replaced with "Former Plan E user". Other travellers made decisions based
on them, and removing them distorts the record.

You may ask for full deletion of specific reviews at [SUPPORT EMAIL], and we will
consider it — particularly where a review contains personal detail about you.

## 7. Hosts

Host accounts additionally require: all upcoming bookings completed or cancelled with
travellers refunded; final payouts settled; listings unpublished.

Retained for **7 years**: verification records, ID reference (not the full document),
payout records, and safety incident records. Public listings are removed at deletion.

## 8. Recovery

Within the 7-day grace period, log in and the deletion is cancelled — everything is
intact.

**After the grace period, deletion is permanent.** We cannot restore the account, its
history, saved experiences, or booking records visible to you. You may sign up again
with the same email, but it will be an empty new account.

## 9. Deleting some data without deleting the account

You do not have to close your account to reduce what we hold. In Settings you can remove
your profile photo, clear saved experiences, turn off location, withdraw marketing
consent, and delete health information from past bookings.

## 10. If we refuse

We will not refuse a valid deletion request. If we cannot act immediately because of
section 2, we say which condition applies, what clears it, and we proceed once it does.

If you believe we have wrongly refused or delayed, complain under the Grievance Policy
or to the **National Information Commission**.

## 11. Deletion by us

We may delete accounts inactive for **[X] years**, after two notices to your registered
email at least 30 days apart.

Accounts closed for breach of the Terms are deactivated but their records are retained
for the periods above, so that a banned user cannot clear their history by requesting
deletion.
