## 1. What we collect

### You give us

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | Sign-up | Account, booking confirmations |
| Password (hashed, never stored in readable form) | Sign-up | Authentication |
| Profile photo, location, bio | Optional, profile | Personalisation |
| Interests | Onboarding | Recommendations |
| Booking contact details, guest counts | Booking | Host manifest, contact |
| Health, dietary, accessibility or fitness information | Optional, at booking | Safety on the experience. See section 4 |
| Emergency contact | High-risk activities | Emergency response |
| Messages to hosts and support | In use | Delivering the service, disputes |
| Reviews and photos | After a trip | Public display |
| Government ID and bank details | Host applications only | Verification, payouts, legal obligation |

### We collect automatically

Device type and OS, app version, IP address, approximate location (only with your
permission, to show nearby experiences), pages and listings viewed, search terms,
crash and diagnostic logs, cookies and similar technologies on the website (see the
Cookie Policy).

### We do not collect

We never see or store your **full card number, CVV, or banking credentials**. Payments
happen inside Khalti or eSewa. We receive only a transaction reference, an amount, and
a success or failure result.

## 2. Why we use it

- To create and run your account
- To take bookings, process payments and issue refunds
- To pass necessary details to the host so they can deliver the experience safely
- To send booking confirmations, reminders and service messages
- To provide customer support and handle grievances
- To keep the platform safe — detecting fraud, abuse and fake reviews
- To improve the product, using aggregated and de-identified data where possible
- To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations
- With your consent, to send marketing. You can withdraw this at any time

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. What we share, and with whom

**With the host you booked** — your name, phone, email, guest count, and any safety
information you provided. Hosts are contractually required to use it only for your
booking and to delete it afterwards.

**With payment providers** — Khalti, eSewa and their banks, to process the transaction.

**With service providers** — Supabase (database and file hosting), notification and
email delivery, analytics and crash reporting. Each is bound to process data only on
our instructions. See section 8 on where data is stored.

**With authorities** — where required by law, court order, or where necessary to
protect someone's life or safety, including search-and-rescue.

**In a business transfer** — if the company is sold or merged, with notice to you.

**Never** to advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Health and other sensitive information

Some experiences ask about medical conditions, allergies, injuries or fitness. This is
sensitive personal data under the Individual Privacy Act, 2075.

- We collect it **only with your explicit consent** and only where it is relevant to
  safety on that activity
- It is shared **only** with the host running that experience, and with medical or
  rescue services in an emergency
- It is deleted **90 days after** the experience ends unless we need it for an ongoing
  incident, insurance claim or legal matter
- You may decline to provide it. The host may then decline your participation on a
  high-risk activity — that is a safety decision, not a penalty

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account profile | While your account is open |
| Booking and payment records | **7 years** after the booking — required for tax and company law |
| Health and emergency contact data | 90 days after the experience |
| Messages | 2 years, or longer if part of a dispute |
| Reviews | Indefinitely (anonymised if you delete your account) |
| Support tickets | 3 years |
| Analytics and logs | 24 months |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn, plus 1 year |

## 6. Your rights

You may:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** anything inaccurate — most of it directly in the app
- **Delete** your account and data, subject to legal retention (see the Account
  Deletion Policy)
- **Object** to processing based on our legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** for marketing, location or health data at any time
- **Export** your data in a machine-readable format
- **Complain** to us at [DPO EMAIL], or to the National Information Commission

We respond within **15 days**, and tell you if we need longer, up to a maximum of 30.
There is no charge unless a request is repetitive or excessive.

## 7. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Passwords hashed. Row-level access controls in
the database so a user can only reach their own records. Access to production data
restricted to staff who need it and logged. Payment data never touches our systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at meaningful risk we will
notify you and the relevant authority **without undue delay and within 72 hours** of
becoming aware.

## 8. Where your data is stored

Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase, on servers in

transferred and stored abroad. We rely on the provider's contractual data protection
commitments to keep the standard of protection equivalent. Ask us at [DPO EMAIL] for
details.

## 9. Children

Plan E is not for under-18s. Accounts are for adults. A guardian may book on behalf of a
minor participant and provides that minor's details themselves. If we learn we have
collected data directly from a child, we delete it.

## 10. Automated decisions

We use automated systems to rank recommendations and to flag suspicious transactions.
We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you by automated
means alone. A flagged transaction is reviewed by a person before any account action.

use local storage and device identifiers for the same purposes.
